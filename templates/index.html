Prevalent Phishing-Link Scams in India

1. Cloned NetBanking Portals (Lookalike Domains)

Attackers host visually identical replicas of banking portals to compromise authentication tokens.

▼
🛑 Mechanism Matrix: Fraudsters distribute text threads stating your banking access expires tonight. The hyperlink opens a cloned portal interface capturing your credentials to immediately execute parallel malicious transactions.
🛡️ Defense Rule: Never use link markers inside text threads to log into financial apps. Official institutional endpoints in India strictly terminate in configurations like .gov.in or .nic.in.

2. PAN / Aadhaar KYC Verification Phishing Traps

Panicky warnings mimic official compliance documentation updates, redirecting victims to harvesting forms.

▼
🛑 Mechanism Matrix: Automated text arrays indicate credit facilities will be permanently frozen unless you immediately complete mandatory document verification steps through an untrusted third-party hyperlink.
🛡️ Defense Rule: Central administrative desks never deploy block warnings containing hidden external routing links. Crosscheck compliance alerts directly inside official corporate client applications.

3. Electricity Disconnection & Logistics Gateway Scams

Deceptive forms scrape consumer credentials under the guise of processing overdue charges or tracking parcels.

▼
🛑 Mechanism Matrix: Malicious alerts demand a token processing payment of ₹10 to avoid service cut-offs. The linked gateway records your debit card parameters and key logs your data patterns.
🛡️ Defense Rule: Public utility infrastructure bodies do not coordinate operations via private messaging networks containing random checkout forms. Always query real bills inside verified utility portals.

Link Fraud Recovery & Support FAQ

I tapped a suspicious link from an SMS string. What actions do I take right now?

Minimize potential damage immediately:

  1. Disconnect networks instantly: Switch your phone onto Airplane Mode immediately to stop background script cycles from transmitting device data profiles to remote hosts.
  2. Clear browser caches, navigation history, and active tracking cookies immediately.
  3. Check your device storage directories to make sure no background applications (like obscure `.apk` packages) were silently deployed.
  4. Change your high-priority banking passwords and access PIN codes using a different, clean machine terminal.
A link prompted me to download a file, and now money is transferring out!

This means your phone has been infected by a malicious screen-mirroring tool or an automated SMS forwarder:

  1. Cut off network signals: Turn off your Wi-Fi router or switch to Airplane Mode immediately to disconnect the attacker.
  2. Look through your installed applications list and remove any untrusted tools (like AnyDesk or QuickSupport variants).
  3. If touch panels lock up, trigger a hardware-level **Factory Reset** to clean out the system core.
  4. Call **1930** immediately from a clean device to instruct local financial enforcement teams to freeze target account lines.
How can I report a fraudulent website domain proactively to secure others?

You can proactively report malicious links to help protect the broader online community:

  • Log onto the Ministry of Communications' central **Chakshu Portal** (hosted on Sanchar Saathi) to flag the sender's details and the associated malicious URL.
  • Forward scam link text formats to the dedicated email desk managed by the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team at **report@cert-in.org.in**.

About The Project

The Core Challenge

Static validation blacklists struggle to intercept modern zero-hour threat structures since attackers continuously change host locations. This platform addresses that bottleneck by parsing 30 unique protocol, lexical, and structural characteristics directly from any suspect domain query.

Machine Learning Architecture

Redirection metrics, subdomain anomalies, and page source characteristics are evaluated using a trained Gradient Boosting Classifier. By dynamically predicting potential safety vulnerabilities on demand, the project delivers predictive intelligence to secure our digital space.
